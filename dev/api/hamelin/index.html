Hamelin et al. (2016)

PowderyMildew.HamelinParameters — Type
HamelinParameters(; beta, chi, alpha = 0.0, S0 = 1.0, season_length = 200.0)

Parameters of the seasonal model of Hamelin et al. (2016) for n = length(beta) parasite species sharing one host: within-season transmission rates beta (per day, per unit leaf density), composite between-season coefficients chi (production of survival forms, their survival and primary-infection success together; not a survival probability), the removal rate alpha (per day), the leaf density S0 renewed each year before primary infection, and the season length season_length (days). The paper's fit uses alpha = 0 and S0 = 1, with species 1 Erysiphe alphitoides and species 2 E. quercicola.

source
PowderyMildew.HamelinProblem — Type
HamelinProblem(p::HamelinParameters, I0; S = p.S0 - sum(I0), seasons = 1)

The model of Hamelin et al. (2016) run for seasons growing seasons, starting from infected leaf densities I0 (one per species) and susceptible density S at the start of the first season. The default S starts the first season as the annual map would, with S + sum(I0) = S0. Solve it with solve.

source
PowderyMildew.HamelinSolution — Type
HamelinSolution

The solution of a HamelinProblem: one dense ODE solution per season, each over days 0 … season_length of that season. Call sol(season, t) for the state [S; I₁ … Iₙ; R] on day t of season, and see frequency and incidence.

PowderyMildew.between_season — Function
between_season(p::HamelinParameters, u)

The annual map of Hamelin et al. (2016): from the state u = [S; I₁ … Iₙ; R] at the end of a season to the state at the start of the next. With H = Σ χᵢ Iᵢ, susceptible leaves start at S0 exp(-H) and species i infects S0 (1 - exp(-H)) χᵢ Iᵢ / H, the limit as H → 0 being no infection; R restarts at zero.

frequency(sol::HamelinSolution, season, t; species = 1)

The share of infected leaves infected by species on day t of season, Iᵢ / Σⱼ Iⱼ (the paper's F for species 1). It is NaN when no leaf is infected.

PowderyMildew.coexistence_chi — Function
coexistence_chi(p::HamelinParameters, I0)

The between-season coefficients χ for which the start-of-season state with infected densities I0 and S = S0 - sum(I0) is a fixed point of one season followed by between_season, given the within-season parameters of p (whose own chi is ignored). Hamelin et al. (2016) inferred χ this way, under a coexistence-equilibrium assumption, rather than from winter observations. With H = -log(1 - Σ I0 / S0) and Iᵢ(τ) the end-of-season densities, χᵢ = H I0ᵢ / (Σⱼ I0ⱼ Iᵢ(τ)).

source
PowderyMildew.frequency_loglikelihood — Function
frequency_loglikelihood(sol::HamelinSolution, season, t, sampled, alphitoides; species = 1)

The binomial log-likelihood of Hamelin et al. (2016): on observation k, alphitoides[k] of the sampled[k] singly infected leaves sampled on day t[k] of season[k] carry species, with probability frequency. sampled counts identified single infections, not all leaves examined. It is -Inf when an observed count is impossible, including when no leaf is infected.
